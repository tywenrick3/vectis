# Video Review Checklist

Use this when reviewing published videos. Run the pipeline 3-5 times, then watch each video critically.

---

## Hook (first 1-3 seconds)

- [ ] Contains a concrete fact, number, or named entity (not a vague question)
- [ ] Would stop you from scrolling if you saw it in your feed
- [ ] Text is readable at phone size
- [ ] Animation draws the eye immediately (spring bounce, slide-in, etc.)

## Visual Cues (per segment)

- [ ] Cue type matches the content (counter for numbers, bar chart for comparisons, etc.)
- [ ] Data is legible — labels, numbers, and bars are large enough at 1080x1920
- [ ] Animations are smooth — no jank, clipping, or overflow
- [ ] Timing feels right — cue appears when the narrator references it, not too early/late
- [ ] Colors are niche-appropriate (tech = cyan, finance = green)
- [ ] Transitions between segments feel natural (no abrupt cuts or long dead time)

## Pacing

- [ ] Total video length is 30-60 seconds (sweet spot for Shorts)
- [ ] No segment feels rushed or drags
- [ ] CTA comes at a natural conclusion, not abruptly

## Audio

- [ ] Voice sounds natural, not robotic or too fast
- [ ] Speed setting (1.1x) feels comfortable — not rushed
- [ ] Pronunciation of technical terms and names is correct
- [ ] Volume is consistent throughout

## Captions

- [ ] Word-by-word timing is synced with audio (no visible lag)
- [ ] Font size is readable on a phone screen
- [ ] Caption position doesn't overlap key visuals
- [ ] No words cut off at screen edges
- [ ] Punctuation and capitalization look natural

## Overall

- [ ] Would you watch this to the end if it appeared in your feed?
- [ ] Does it teach something specific (not vague/generic)?
- [ ] Would you tap "like" or leave a comment?
- [ ] Anything feel obviously AI-generated in a bad way?
